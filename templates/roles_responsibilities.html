assignment Role Descriptions & Responsibilities

POSITION 1

Sarpanch (President)

Elected Head
  • Highest responsibility in the Gram Panchayath
  • Chairs all meetings and holds overall executive authority
  • Exercises financial control jointly with the Up-Sarpanch
  • Represents the GP to higher authorities
  • Ensures implementation of all resolutions
POSITION 2

Up-Sarpanch (Vice-President)

Elected
  • Assumes all responsibilities of the Sarpanch in their absence
  • Chairs specific committees
  • Shares financial signatory power
  • Assists in coordinating development activities
POSITION 3

Secretary

Elected
  • Administrative head and highest staff responsibility
  • Implements all decisions and manages funds
  • Maintains records and official documentation
  • Coordinates government schemes and programs
  • Acts as the link to the state government machinery

Supporting Staff Positions

Clerical/Assistant Staff

Supports the Secretary with daily office administration, data entry, and record maintenance.

Bill Collector / Tax Collector

Responsible for collecting local taxes, rates, and fees to generate GP revenue.

Operational Staff

Handles water supply maintenance, sanitation, and waste management operations.

Panchs (Ward Members)

Represent wards, participate in decision-making, and work within standing committees.
