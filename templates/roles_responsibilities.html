Constitutional Offices & Responsibilities

Charter Specifications
Office 01 Elected President

Adhyaksha (Sarpanch)

Chief political executive of the Gram Panchayath elected by ward representatives.

  • Presides over all Gram Sabha assemblies and executive panchayat meetings.
  • Exercises executive supervision over development works, village water, and health schemes.
  • Joint signatory on statutory panchayat funds alongside the Executive Secretary/PDO.
  • Represents the Gram Panchayat at Taluk and Zilla Panchayat review conferences.
Office 02 Vice President

Upadhyaksha (Up-Sarpanch)

Deputy executive assuming presidential authority during absence or delegation.

  • Discharges all presidential duties during the temporary absence or leave of the Adhyaksha.
  • Chairs designated standing committees (Social Justice, Civic Amenities, or Production).
  • Coordinates inter-ward developmental priorities and grievance hearings.
  • Inspects ongoing public works and certifies completion milestones.
Office 03 Statutory Official

Secretary / PDO

Government-appointed Panchayat Development Officer and administrative chief.

  • Custodian of official records, minute books, citizen registers, and public funds.
  • Executes statutory resolutions passed by the Gram Panchayath and state circulars.
  • Adjudicates citizen service applications, building licenses, and grievance escalations.
  • Submits quarterly budget audits to the Taluk Executive Officer.
